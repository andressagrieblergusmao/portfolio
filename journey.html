Undergraduate research

Scientific projects at UTFPR

a

Land use and soil texture effects on water retention in the Amazon

Remote analysis of 160 soil samples using RETC and the van Genuchten model, comparing forest and pasture, sandy and clayey soils.

b

Soil physical limits to soybean root elongation

Systematic review following PRISMA guidelines; 8 core studies selected from three databases.

  • Review article
c

Water quality monitoring in the Marrecas River

Sampled nine points for metals using TXRF; found exceedances of CONAMA 357/2005 limits; statistical analysis with ANOVA, Tukey and PCA.

  • PhD research (Liliane Hellmann)
  • SICITE/UTFPR 2019 conference paper
