Tutorial

Mapping areas with QGIS

Objective: import the Google Earth allotment into QGIS and compute areas and lengths, with proper symbology.

1–11 Workflow

  1. Import the KML/KMZ file from Google Earth.
  2. Add an OpenStreetMap basemap with the QuickMapServices plugin.
  3. Calculate the area of each block.
  4. Review the statistics.
  5. Measure the streets.
  6. Style the layers.
  7. Split the UR and UM blocks.
  8. Apply graduated and categorized symbology.
  9. Add the Google Satellite basemap.
  10. Digitize extra polygons.
  11. Create a main-road line layer.

The final map combines area classes and land-use categories.
